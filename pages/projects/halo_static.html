Project: Halo Static

Top-Down Rogue-Lite Demon Slaying

Mission Brief

Step into the shoes of an angel-possessed doll navigating the echoing chambers of an ancient spire. Explore a procedurally generated 20-room dungeon, master dual-weapon combat, defeat corrupted entities, and gather the key required to face the ultimate boss.

Active Gameplay

Active gameplay. User can choose between two weapons, a saber or an orb. Orb fires quickly, saber slashes with more power. Portrait art made by me as well.

Key Highlights

Procedural 20-Room Dungeon Generator

Dynamically builds a 4x5 grid layout with rotation-aware door transitions, a guaranteed key vault, and a locked boss room requiring 30+ enemy kills and the dungeon key to breach.

Dual-Weapon Combat & Harmonic System

Equip the close-range Saber or fire projectiles with the Orb of Light. Combat generates Harmonic Energy, allowing players to unleash a devastating screen-clearing Harmonic Blast when fully charged.

Custom Dialogue & Hybrid UI

Features a specialized typewriter text box with distinct antagonist/protagonist styling, real-time HUD trackers for health, harmony, and unique room exploration metrics.
